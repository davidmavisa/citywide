FAQ

Good questions

Do you bring your own equipment?

Yes. We bring professional DJ gear, speakers and lighting sized for your event. If your boat or venue already has a sound system, we can plug straight into it.

How far ahead should I book?

Weekends from November to March book out early, especially around Christmas. Get in touch as soon as you have a date. We can sometimes help at short notice too.

Can I choose the music?

Absolutely. We'll share a Spotify playlist for your event. Add your must-plays, invite your friends to add theirs, and let us know anything you don't want to hear. We'll mix it with crowd favourites and keep the dance floor full.

Where do you meet us for a boat party?

At the wharf. We arrive before boarding and set up on the vessel, so everything is ready when your guests step on.

How much does a DJ cost?

It depends on the date, length of the event and setup needed. Send us your details and we'll reply with a clear quote.
